Game rules

A round lasts 300 seconds from its first message. Your last message in that round determines your group. The uniquely largest group wins: each winner gets floor(losers ÷ winners) points. A tie for largest group gives no points. A group of at least 25 players saying reset at round end resets all scores. Automatic reset messages are blocked.
